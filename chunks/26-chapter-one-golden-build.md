Goal: Preserve the first complete version of Stormlight Village Chapter 1 after a successful full-route playthrough.

## Milestone

Stormlight Village now has a playable six-scene first chapter that feels complete end to end.

The chapter loop is:

1. Walk through a storm-soaked village route.
2. Notice a damaged piece of local infrastructure.
3. Let the robot scan the repair point.
4. Solve a short themed tile-rotation puzzle.
5. Hear and see the repair payoff.
6. Share a brief reward beat with nearby characters.
7. Move into the next scene under continuous rain and storm ambience.
8. Finish at Rainbarrel Row with a chapter-complete moment.
