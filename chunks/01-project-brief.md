# Chunk 01 - Project Brief

Status: Complete draft

Goal: Summarize the current creative direction from the 25-question planning file.

Why this matters: This keeps the project anchored while we work in smaller chunks.

## Decisions

- Working title: Stormlight Village.
- Game type: cozy 2D side-scrolling adventure platformer.
- First deliverable: a browser-based visual mockup scene with no gameplay yet.
- First technical direction: plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript organized for expansion.
- Main character: a young apprentice civil engineer.
- Character choice: more masculine-presenting or more feminine-presenting appearance only, with the same story and abilities.
- Companion: a cute floating AI companion robot.
- Setting: whimsical forest village with handmade machines and nature reclaiming old inventions.
- Opening setup: the apprentice wakes after a heavy storm and finds the village water wheel generator damaged.
- Mood: calm after the storm, with damp forest, soft light, gentle mystery, and cozy recovery.
- Core future loop: explore, collect missing repair parts, use the lantern, repair the water wheel, restore lights, and open a path.

## Notes

The game should feel practical and heartfelt. The character is not just inventing for spectacle; they are repairing village infrastructure after storm damage. The world should show the storm's impact while keeping the experience gentle and hopeful.
