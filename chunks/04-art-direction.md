# Chunk 04 - Art Direction

Status: Ready

Goal: Document the look and feel for Stormlight Village.

Why this matters: The project depends on a consistent art direction before AI-generated PNG assets are created later.

## Style

Whimsical hand-drawn storybook style with soft colors, clean silhouettes, cozy detail, and readable game shapes.

## Character

The main character is a young apprentice civil engineer. The player should eventually choose between a more masculine-presenting and more feminine-presenting version. Both versions share the same story, role, abilities, and animations where possible.

Visual cues:

- Engineer cap.
- Practical overalls.
- Polished tool kit.
- Lantern gadget.
- Tidy, capable, apprentice-like design.

## Companion Robot

The companion is a cute floating AI robot. It should feel friendly, handmade, and helpful.

Visual cues:

- Small round body.
- Expressive screen or eye.
- Tiny antenna.
- Soft glow.
- Gentle hover posture.

## World

Stormlight Village is a whimsical forest village with handmade machines and nature reclaiming old inventions.

Visual cues:

- Water wheel generator.
- Wooden platforms and bridges.
- Lamps and cables.
- Mossy gears and old mechanisms.
- Wet leaves, puddles, mist, and fallen branches after a heavy storm.

## Color And Mood

The mood is calm after the storm. Use damp greens, warm lamp light, soft brass/copper machine colors, misty blue-gray shadows, and small golden lantern accents.

## Next Steps

- Use this file to guide the first visual mockup.
- Later, use it to write AI image prompts for character, robot, environment, and water wheel assets.
