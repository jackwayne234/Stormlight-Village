# Chunk 11 - Fourth Scene: Stormedge Rise

Status: First pass complete

Goal: Add a fourth standalone scene where the character has moved closer to the storm.

Why this matters: Stormedge Rise gives the world a weather arc. Earlier scenes feel like the aftermath; this scene feels like the edge of the storm is still active.

## Scene

Stormedge Rise

## Page

`stormedge-rise.html`

## Direction

A wind-battered forest ridge with heavier rain, faster mist, wind-blown leaves, storm posts, a weather gauge, puddles, lamps, and distant storm energy.

## What Was Added

- Separate browser page for Scene 04.
- Scene data in `src/scenes/stormedgeRiseScene.js`.
- Scene-specific storm timing: lightning/thunder can happen more often here than in calmer scenes.
- Renderer support for heavier rain, wind leaves, storm posts, and a storm gauge.

## Controls

- `A` or Left Arrow: walk left.
- `D` or Right Arrow: walk right.

No jumping, no vertical movement.

## Notes

No scene-to-scene connection yet. This is intentionally another standalone walk-around scene.
