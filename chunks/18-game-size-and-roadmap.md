# Chunk 18 - Game Size and Roadmap

Status: Direction captured

Goal: Keep Stormlight Village at the right size for the current stage and avoid expanding before the core chapter feels polished.

## Current Size

Stormlight Village is currently best treated as a small playable vertical slice:

- 6 connected scenes
- 1 repair per scene
- simple left/right movement
- scene-specific repair puzzles
- reward moments after repairs
- rain and thunder ambience
- a chapter-complete ending

This is enough to feel like a complete first chapter. The next work should improve pacing, feel, and polish rather than adding more scenes immediately.

## Scope Decision

Do not expand the route yet.

The current priority is to make the six-scene route charming and complete:

- smoother scene transitions
- better reward pacing
- clearer repair feedback
- sound cues for scan, rotation, success, and scene payoffs
- a small intro/outro if needed
- full playthrough review from start to chapter complete

## Future Game Shape

If the prototype grows into a fuller game, aim for a compact chapter structure rather than a huge open world.

Possible long-term shape:

1. Chapter 1: Stormlight Village recovery
   - Current six-scene route.
   - Focus: restore village infrastructure after the storm.

2. Chapter 2: deeper forest and river systems
   - Forest paths, wetland channels, old bridges, water controls, and nature-powered machines.
   - Focus: repair the systems that connect the village to the surrounding landscape.

3. Chapter 3: storm tower and mountain weather station
   - Higher elevation, stronger storm signals, weather instruments, beacon systems, and final storm-preparation repair.
   - Focus: help the village prepare for the next big storm.

## Design Rule

Expand only after the core loop feels good.

The game should feel cozy, practical, and heartfelt, not large for the sake of being large. Better to have a polished short chapter than a sprawling unfinished one.
